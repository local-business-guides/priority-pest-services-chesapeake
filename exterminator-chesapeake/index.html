A simple checklist before you call

You can get ready by gathering a few details:

  • The location of each sighting, such as a room, attic, or crawl space.
  • The date or approximate time you first noticed activity.
  • Whether you’ve seen pests more than once or in multiple areas.
  • Photos of visible signs, if you can take them safely.
  • Any related moisture, insulation, or wood damage concerns.

Skip the guesswork when describing the pest. If you don’t know what it is, a plain description is more helpful than a confident but uncertain label. Don’t disturb a suspected nest or combine treatments without advice. If you’re finding repeated activity or signs that may involve damage, contact a professional to discuss an inspection.
