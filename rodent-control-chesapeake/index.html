Decide when to ask for help

Rodent Control Chesapeake may be worth discussing after you spot a rat or mouse, or find something that makes you suspect one has been inside. You do not have to know the animal’s route or point of entry before calling. Share what you noticed and let the pest professional help assess what kind of service fits.

Prepare for a useful visit

You can make the appointment easier by gathering a few observations, not by searching every hidden corner. Tell the team where you saw an animal or noticed a possible sign, and mention when it happened. If you have a photo taken from a safe distance, you can show it. Do not handle a rodent or suspected nesting material.

Before the visit, consider this checklist:

  • Make a note of the rooms or parts of the home where you noticed activity.
  • Keep track of dates and whether the concern happened more than once.
  • Make the areas you want checked accessible, where practical.
  • Ask what the service includes and what you can do between visits.

Avoid using a treatment or setting a trap without understanding whether it is suitable for your household. Also avoid assuming that one sighting tells the whole story. A professional assessment can help you decide what to do next.
