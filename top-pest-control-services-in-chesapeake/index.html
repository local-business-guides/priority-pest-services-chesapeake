How to compare service options

A useful comparison is based on what each company says it will do, not just a broad service label. Keep this checklist handy when you call:

  • Explain the pest, location, and signs you’ve noticed.
  • Ask what the inspection or service will cover.
  • Find out whether pest management or maintenance should be considered.
  • Mention any moisture, insulation, or structural concerns.
  • Ask what you should do before the appointment.

Avoid assuming that one treatment is right for every pest problem. Don’t hide previous work or products you’ve used, either. That information gives the service provider a more complete picture. If a recommendation is unclear, ask for it to be explained in everyday terms before you decide how to proceed.
