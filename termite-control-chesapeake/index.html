Get a clearer picture of the termite concern

Termite Control Chesapeake is a service to consider when you have noticed possible insect activity or are worried about damage around wood in your home. Before the visit, jot down where you noticed the issue and when you first saw it. If you have photos, keep them handy. Avoid pulling apart or repairing a suspected area before it can be viewed, since that may make it harder to understand what you found.

What to expect from the service

The process should be based on what is found at your home. You can expect to explain the areas that concern you, have the property inspected, and discuss the pest service or follow-up that may be appropriate. Ask what areas were checked, what evidence supports the assessment, and whether another visit or repair discussion is recommended.

A short preparation list can help:

  • Note the rooms, crawl space areas or exterior spots where you saw a change.
  • Write down whether the concern has appeared once or more than once.
  • Make access available to the areas you want inspected.
  • Ask whether any suggested repair is separate from pest treatment.

Do not assume every damaged or discolored piece of wood points to termites. Let the inspection guide the conversation, and ask for a plain explanation if a finding is unclear.
